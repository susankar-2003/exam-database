1. Which is the most popular folk dance of Assam, celebrated during the arrival of the Assamese New Year?

A) Bihu Dance (বিহু নৃত্য)
B) Bagurumba
C) Jhumur
D) Sattriya
Ans: A

2. Bagurumba is the traditional folk dance of which tribe of Assam?
