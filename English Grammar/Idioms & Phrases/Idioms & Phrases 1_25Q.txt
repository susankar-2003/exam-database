1. The idiom "Once in a blue moon" means:

A) Very frequently
B) Very rarely
C) On full moon days
D) Never
Ans: B

2. What is the meaning of "To beat around the bush"?

A) To cut plants in a garden
B) To avoid coming to the main point
C) To speak directly and frankly
D) To hide in a forest
Ans: B

3. "At the eleventh hour" means:

A) At 11 o'clock sharp
B) At the last possible moment
C) Early in the morning
D) In the middle of the night
Ans: B

4. "Apple of one's eye" refers to:

A) Someone who loves eating apples
B) Someone very dear and cherished
C) A person with poor eyesight
D) An enemy
Ans: B

5. To "Bite the bullet" means:

A) To eat something hard
B) To face a difficult situation courageously
C) To get shot in a fight
D) To avoid responsibilities
Ans: B

6. "Break the ice" means:

A) To freeze liquid water
B) To start a conversation and ease tension
C) To cause damage to something
D) To cool down a drink
Ans: B

7. "Burn the midnight oil" means:

A) To waste fuel
B) To work or study late into the night
C) To set something on fire
D) To sleep early
Ans: B

8. To "Call it a day" means:

A) To name a specific day
B) To stop working on something for the rest of the day
C) To start a new project
D) To invite someone for a visit
Ans: B

9. What does "Cry over spilt milk" mean?

A) To feel sad about dropping food
B) To regret something that cannot be changed
C) To clean up a mess quickly
D) To complain about poor service
Ans: B

10. "A piece of cake" means:

A) A sweet dessert
B) Something very easy to do
C) A small share of profit
D) A difficult challenge
Ans: B

11. To "Add fuel to the fire" means:

A) To cook food on an open flame
B) To make a bad situation worse
C) To calm down angry people
D) To restart an engine
Ans: B

12. "As fit as a fiddle" means:

A) In very good physical health
B) Able to play musical instruments
C) Very weak and frail
D) Highly energetic only in the morning
Ans: A

13. "Back to the drawing board" means:

A) To paint a picture
B) To start again from the beginning after a failure
C) To clean a classroom board
D) To review an old success
Ans: B

14. To "Bark up the wrong tree" means:

A) To shout at animals
B) To pursue a mistaken line of thought or action
C) To plant trees in the wrong place
D) To make a lot of noise
Ans: B

15. "A blessing in disguise" means:

A) A bad event that turns out to have good results
B) A disguised priest
C) A curse given by an enemy
D) Something that brings immediate wealth
Ans: A

16. "Burn the candle at both ends" means:

A) To save energy
B) To exhaust oneself by overworking
C) To celebrate a festival
D) To light up a dark room
Ans: B

17. To achieve something "By hook or by crook" means:

A) By honest work only
B) By any means necessary, fair or foul
C) Through legal procedures
D) With the help of friends
Ans: B

18. "Cast pearls before swine" means:

A) To feed precious food to animals
B) To offer valuable things to people who do not appreciate them
C) To waste money on shopping
D) To decorate an animal
Ans: B

19. To "Catch someone red-handed" means:

A) To catch someone with painted hands
B) To catch someone in the act of doing something wrong
C) To injure someone's hand
D) To arrest a criminal after several years
Ans: B

20. A "Cock and bull story" means:

A) An interesting fable about animals
B) An absurd and unbelievable story
C) A story told by farmers
D) A true historical event
Ans: B

21. A "Dark horse" refers to:

A) A black horse used in racing
B) An unexpected winner or competitor with hidden talents
C) A person who works in the dark
D) A wicked person
Ans: B

22. To "Die in harness" means:

A) To die in a battle
B) To die while still in active service or working
C) To die of an incurable disease
D) To die in an accident
Ans: B

23. A "Fair-weather friend" is:

A) A friend who helps in all seasons
B) A friend who supports you only during good times
C) A friend who loves outdoor weather
D) A lifelong loyal companion
Ans: B

24. To "Feather one's own nest" means:

A) To build a house for birds
B) To make oneself rich, often dishonestly
C) To decorate one's bedroom
D) To buy comfortable furniture
Ans: B

25. "Gift of the gab" means:

A) Ability to speak fluently and persuasively
B) Talent for singing
C) Receiving unexpected presents
D) Ability to write poetry
Ans: A
