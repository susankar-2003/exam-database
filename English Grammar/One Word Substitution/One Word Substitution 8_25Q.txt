1. A disease that spreads over a whole country or the world:

A) Endemic
B) Epidemic
C) Pandemic
D) Sporadic
Ans: C

2. A disease regularly found among particular people or in a certain area:

A) Endemic
B) Pandemic
C) Epidemic
D) Infectious
Ans: A

3. A disease affecting a large number of people within a community at one time:

A) Pandemic
B) Epidemic
C) Endemic
D) Chronic
Ans: B

4. A person who wanders from place to place without a fixed home:

A) Vagrant
B) Resident
C) Citizen
D) Native
Ans: A

5. A remedy for counteracting the effects of a disease:

A) Panacea
B) Cure
C) Antidote
D) Sedative
Ans: B

6. Animals that give birth to live young and nurse them with milk:

A) Reptiles
B) Mammals
C) Birds
D) Insects
