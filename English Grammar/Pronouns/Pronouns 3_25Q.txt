1. "Who is that standing near the gate?" What type of pronoun is "Who"?

A) Relative Pronoun
B) Interrogative Pronoun
C) Demonstrative Pronoun
D) Indefinite Pronoun
Ans: B

2. "This is my bicycle." What type of pronoun is "This"?

A) Demonstrative Pronoun
B) Possessive Pronoun
C) Personal Pronoun
D) Relative Pronoun
Ans: A

3. "These are fresh apples." Here "These" functions as a:

A) Demonstrative Adjective
B) Demonstrative Pronoun
C) Relative Pronoun
D) Indefinite Pronoun
Ans: B

4. "None of the guests have arrived." Here "None" is a/an:

A) Personal Pronoun
B) Indefinite Pronoun
C) Distributive Pronoun
D) Relative Pronoun
Ans: B

5. "Each of the boys was rewarded." Here "Each" is a/an:

A) Distributive Pronoun
B) Indefinite Pronoun
C) Relative Pronoun
D) Personal Pronoun
Ans: A

6. Neither of the proposals ____ accepted.

A) was
B) were
C) have been
D) are
Ans: A

7. Either you or he has forgotten ____ umbrella.

A) your
B) his
C) their
D) our
Ans: B

8. You, he and I must do ____ duty.

A) your
B) his
C) our
D) my
Ans: C

9. You and he must complete ____ task today.

A) your
B) his
C) our
D) my
Ans: A

10. He and I ____ friends since childhood.

A) am
B) is
C) are
D) was
Ans: C

11. Correct order of personal pronouns when confessing a fault is:

A) You, he and I
B) I, you and he
C) He, you and I
D) You, I and he
Ans: B

12. Correct order of personal pronouns in normal general sentences is:

A) I, you and he
B) You, he and I
C) He, you and I
D) You, I and he
Ans: B

13. When plural personal pronouns are used together, the correct order is:

A) We, you and they
B) You, we and they
C) They, you and we
D) We, they and you
Ans: A

14. "I, you and he committed a mistake." This sentence follows the rule of:

A) 231
B) 123
C) 321
D) 312

B) It is I who did it.
C) It is myself who did it.

C) One must keep their promise.
