1. The officer along with his assistants finished ____ work on time.

A) their
B) his

D) its

A) his
B) their
C) its
D) her
Ans: B

3. Neither the secretaries nor the minister gave ____ consent.

A) their
B) his
C) its
D) our
Ans: B

4. Everyone raised ____ voice against corruption.

A) their
B) his

D) one's
Ans: B

5. Either Ram or his friends lost ____ tickets.

A) his
B) their
C) its
D) our
Ans: B

6. The flock of sheep was moving, and ____ shepherd was following it.

A) their
B) its
C) his
D) her
Ans: B

B) a whole single body

D) divided units

A) its
B) their

D) her
Ans: B

A) Yours sincerely
B) Your's sincerely
C) Yours' sincerely

A) comparison
B) selection

24. "Who is there in the room?" Here "Who" is a/an:

A) Relative Pronoun

25. "Whatever happens, happens for good." "Whatever" is a:

A) Compound Relative Pronoun
