1. The Peloponnesian War was fought between which two Greek city-states?

A) Athens and Sparta (এথেঞ্চ আৰু স্পাৰ্টা)
B) Athens and Troy (এথেঞ্চ আৰু ট্ৰয়)
C) Sparta and Corinth (স্পাৰ্টা আৰু কোৰিন্থ)
D) Thebes and Athens (থিবছ আৰু এথেঞ্চ)
