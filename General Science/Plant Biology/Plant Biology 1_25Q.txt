1. Which organelle is known as the powerhouse of the cell?

A) Ribosome ( ৰাইব’জ’ম )
B) Mitochondria ( মাইট’কণ্ড্ৰিয়া )
C) Lysosome ( লাইছ’জ’ম )
D) Chloroplast ( ক্ল’ৰ’প্লাষ্ট )
Ans: B

4. What is the green pigment in plants responsible for capturing light energy?
