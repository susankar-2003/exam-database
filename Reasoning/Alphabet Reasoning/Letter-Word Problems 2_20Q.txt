1. In the word "BREAKTHROUGH", if all the vowels are removed, which letter will be 5th from the right end?

A) K 
B) T 
C) H 
D) R 
Ans: B

2. If the letters of the word "VERTICAL" are arranged alphabetically, how many letters will remain unchanged in their position?

A) None
B) One 
C) Two 
D) Three Ans: A

3. How many meaningful English words can be formed using the 2nd, 4th, and 6th letters of the word "SILENT"?

A) One

C) Three 
D) None

A) 3
B) 4
